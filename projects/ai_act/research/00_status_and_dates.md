## The amendment that changed the timeline

The AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — was amended in 2026 by the **"Digital Omnibus on AI"**,
reported as **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** *(number seen in two secondary sources; confirm on
EUR-Lex before stating it aloud)*.

| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Commission tabled the Digital Omnibus on AI | 19 November 2025 |
| Provisional trilogue agreement | 7 May 2026 |
| European Parliament final approval | 16 June 2026 |

**Why it happened** is itself an episode: by late 2025 implementation was visibly off track,
principally because the harmonised standards the high-risk regime depends on were not going to
exist in time. The law was moved to fit the standards, not the other way round.

## What the Omnibus added to Article 5

Two new prohibited practices, in force **2 December 2026**:

1. **Non-consensual intimate content.** AI systems that generate or manipulate realistic images,
   video or audio depicting an identifiable person's intimate parts, or that person in sexually
   explicit activity, without freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous and explicit consent.
2. **AI-generated child sexual abuse material.**

The scope point that matters for teaching: for **providers**, the prohibition reportedly extends
beyond systems *intended* for such use to any system where such generation is a **reasonably
foreseeable and reproducible outcome** — so providers of general-purpose image and video tools must
assess foreseeable misuse at design and deployment. *(Secondary-source characterisation; check the
operative wording.)*

## Penalties

Up to **€35 million or 7% of total worldwide annual turnover**, whichever is higher, for prohibited
practices. Lower tiers apply to other breaches — **not yet established in this docket; a gap.**

## GPAI, concretely

- **Code of Practice** published **10 July 2025**; declared adequate **1 August 2025**.
- Three chapters: **Transparency, Copyright, Safety and Security**. The Safety and Security chapter
  binds only providers of models with systemic risk.
- **Systemic-risk threshold: 10^25 FLOP** of training compute — reported as capturing roughly
  **5–15 companies worldwide**.
- **20+ providers** had signed the Code by August 2026.
- Providers of systemic-risk models must notify the AI Office.

## Enforcement architecture

- **AI Office** (inside the Commission) supervises GPAI models centrally and steers uniform
  implementation.
- **National competent authorities**: each Member State designates at least one **notifying
  authority** and one **market-surveillance authority** (Art. 70); market surveillance does
  post-market checks (Art. 74).
- **No confirmed first enforcement action, investigation or fine had been established at the time
  this docket was compiled.** If an episode wants to say what enforcement looks like in practice,
  that is a gap to fill — see `04_gaps.md`.
