Assembled from the episode transcripts, the series plan and the research docket. **The
episodes carry no spoken source list**: a bibliography read aloud is unusable to someone
who is travelling, so the sourcing lives here instead. Regenerate with
`prosodia references --project <dir>`.

Provisions are listed in the order each episode first names them. Where an item the plan
relies on could not be found in `research/`, it is marked so rather than omitted.

## Episode 6 — Risk Against Rights

**People, bodies and instruments named aloud**

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, CEN, CENELEC, JTC 21, Michael Veale, Frederik Zuiderveen Borgesius.

**What the episode was built on** *(from the plan)*

EDPB–EDPS Joint Opinion 5/2021, 18 June 2021. Veale and Zuiderveen Borgesius, *Computer Law Review International* 22(4) (2021) 97–112. COM(2021) 206 final. **And, for the two positions this episode could not previously source — see `research/09_the_choice_of_instrument.md`:** the Commission's own Impact Assessment, **SWD(2021) 84 final, 21 April 2021**, which enumerates the five options assessed — a voluntary labelling scheme; **a sectoral, 'ad-hoc' approach**; a horizontal instrument for high-risk only; that plus codes of conduct for the rest; and mandatory requirements for all AI regardless of risk. **Position 1 is the fourth of those and position 4 is the second**, which means the road not taken is documented by the body that did not take it. For position 4's contemporary holder: **Martin Ebers**, *Truly Risk-Based Regulation of Artificial Intelligence*, European Journal of Risk Regulation — who argues the Act is not truly risk-based *by its own standard* and proposes sector-specific assessment. For position 1's defence as a design: **Ronit Justo-Hanani**, *Policy Design and Practice* 9(2) (2026) 171–180.
